Is there a free version?+

Yes. Downloading and creating a verified profile is always free — membership tiers unlock unlimited matching, video intros, and visibility features.

Does membership auto-renew?+

No. Every plan is a one-time purchase for a fixed term. When it ends, you'll get a reminder to renew — nothing is charged automatically.

Can I change tiers later?+

Yes, at any time from your account settings. Upgrading applies the new tier immediately; the remaining time on your current plan carries over.

What does verification actually check?+

We confirm a government ID against your profile photos and age, so the person you're falling for is exactly who they say they are. See our Safety page for details.

How do refunds work?+

Reach out within 48 hours of purchase if you haven't used any premium features and we'll issue a full refund. See our Terms of Service for the complete policy.
