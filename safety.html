How we verify

Three checks, before
anyone sees your profile.

✓ Identity verification

A government-issued ID is checked against your profile photos and age. Profiles that don't pass never go live, and your ID is never shown to other members.

✓ Employment verification

Confirmed through a work email domain or LinkedIn profile. This is what powers the "Verified" badge you see on other members' cards.

✓ Education verification

Degrees are cross-checked against institutional records where available. Unverifiable claims are simply not displayed.

✓ Photo authenticity

Automated checks catch stock photos, screenshots, and duplicate images across the platform before a profile is approved.

Meeting in person

A few habits worth keeping

  • Video-intro first, and stay in-app messaging until you're comfortable sharing more.
  • Meet the first time in a public place, and get there and back separately.
  • Tell a friend where you're going, who you're meeting, and share your live location if you can.
  • Trust a bad feeling — you're never obligated to stay on a date that feels wrong.
  • Report anything that concerns you, even if you're unsure it "counts." We'd rather look into it.
