The journey

Four steps to falling
for the right person.

No infinite feed, no guessing who's real. Here's exactly what happens between download and your first spark.

01

Show up as yourself

Verify your identity with a government ID — it takes most members under three minutes and happens once. It means the connection you feel is with someone real, not a photo.

02

Say what you're really after

Answer direct questions most apps never ask: are you looking for something long-term, are you open to relocating for love, do you want kids. These answers shape your matches — they're never used to label your profile publicly.

03

Receive a daily shortlist

Each morning, Craze sends a small set of matches selected for real compatibility — values, goals, and chemistry, not just proximity and a photo. You'll never scroll an endless deck; the point is a shortlist worth reading closely.

04

Meet — starting with a video hello

Before numbers are exchanged, send a short video reply. You'll hear their voice and feel their warmth before committing an evening to it. When you're both ready, Craze suggests a public spot near you both.
